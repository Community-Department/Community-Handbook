SECTION 01 // DEPARTMENT INTRODUCTION

Introduction

The Community Department (CD) of Fort Tedder Management operates as the primary liaison between CUSA administration and the general player base. Established to maintain discipline, foster engagement, and ensure seamless communication, the department functions as an essential component of Fort Tedder's operational infrastructure.

DEPARTMENT SCOPE:
The Community Department oversees all non-military community interactions including Discord moderation, in-game support ticket resolution, community event coordination, giveaway administration, and rank progression logging for eligible personnel.

All Community Department personnel are expected to conduct themselves with the highest standards of professionalism. As representatives of CUSA and Fort Tedder Management, your actions reflect directly upon the organization. Maintain neutrality, enforce rules consistently, and prioritize the community experience above personal interests.

CORE VALUES
  • Integrity in all moderation actions
  • Transparency in decision-making
  • Consistency across all platforms
  • Professionalism in public conduct
  • Accountability to Senior Management
OPERATIONAL PLATFORMS
  • Fort Tedder Discord Server
  • In-Game Support Systems
  • CUSA Official Group Wall
  • Community Event Servers
  • Internal Staff Channels
SECTION 02 // OPERATIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

Responsibilities

The following responsibilities are assigned by rank and must be fulfilled to maintain active status within the Community Department. Failure to meet these obligations may result in disciplinary action or removal from the department.

COMMUNITY MODERATOR
  • Monitor and moderate Discord channels according to CUSA Code of Conduct
  • Process and respond to community support tickets within 24 hours
  • Document all moderation actions in official mod logs
  • Report escalated issues to Senior Community Moderators or above
  • Participate in at least one community event per month
  • Maintain activity requirements as specified in quota section
SENIOR COMMUNITY MODERATOR
  • Oversee moderation actions of Community Moderators
  • Handle escalated tickets and appeals from standard moderation
  • Train and mentor newly appointed Community Moderators
  • Coordinate event setup with Community Secretary oversight
  • Review and approve mod log entries for accuracy
  • Act as shift lead during peak activity hours when assigned
COMMUNITY SECRETARY
  • Manage department documentation and handbook updates
  • Oversee all quota tracking and monthly reporting
  • Coordinate inter-department communication and scheduling
  • Process rank log submissions for Community Moderator promotions
  • Organize giveaway logistics and prize distribution
  • Report directly to Community Director with weekly briefings
COMMUNITY DIRECTOR
  • Strategic oversight of all Community Department operations
  • Final authority on all moderation appeals and staff disciplinary actions
  • Direct liaison to Fort Tedder Senior Management and Staff Overseers
  • Approval authority for all rank logs including Secretary and Sr. Moderator
  • Budget and resource allocation for events and giveaways
  • Department recruitment and termination decisions
SECTION 03 // COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Events & Giveaways

Community events and giveaways are essential tools for maintaining player retention and fostering positive relations between CUSA administration and the Fort Tedder player base. All events require proper authorization and documentation.

EVENT PROTOCOLS

01 // EVENT AUTHORIZATION

All community events must be submitted for approval via the Events Request Form at least 72 hours prior to the scheduled date. Senior Community Moderators may approve minor events; major events require Community Director authorization.

02 // EVENT TYPES

Authorized event categories include: Training Demonstrations, Q&A Sessions, Base Tours, Mini-Games, Holiday Celebrations, and Recruitment Drives. Combat-oriented events require additional MP and SOCOM coordination.

03 // DOCUMENTATION

Post-event reports must be filed within 48 hours including attendance numbers, screenshots, incident reports (if any), and community feedback summary. Reports are submitted to the Community Secretary for archival.

GIVEAWAY PROCEDURES

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

• Must be CUSA group member
• Account age minimum 30 days
• No active moderation strikes
• One entry per person per giveaway
• Staff members excluded from entry
APPROVAL PROCESS

1. Request submitted to Community Secretary
2. Prize value verified against budget
3. Community Director approval obtained
4. Giveaway announcement drafted
5. Random winner selection documented
6. Prize distribution logged
NOTE: All giveaway winners must be publicly announced in the designated channel with timestamp verification. Private giveaways are prohibited without explicit Senior Management approval.

RANK LOGS

Community Secretary and Community Director are responsible for maintaining accurate rank logs. These include:

• Promotion recommendations with justification
• Demotion documentation with incident references
• Department transfer records
• Probationary period tracking
• Resignation processing and archival

Rank logs must be updated within 24 hours of any personnel change and submitted to the Staff Overseer office for final approval on all Senior Community Moderator+ appointments.
QUOTA EXEMPTIONS: Exemptions may be granted for approved leave, technical outages, or emergency situations. Requests must be submitted to the Community Secretary at least 48 hours in advance (when possible) with full documentation. Retroactive exemptions require Community Director approval.
